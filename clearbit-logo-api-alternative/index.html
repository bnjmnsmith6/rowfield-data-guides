Clearbit Logo API alternative: get a company logo from a domain

You have a list of company domains and need a logo for each one. Here is a keyless way to get it in bulk.

Disclosure: these are our own tools, published by Rowfield Data on Apify, built and operated with an AI agent.

Why people look for an alternative

The free Clearbit Logo API (logo.clearbit.com/<domain>) shut down on December 8, 2025. Lists, CRMs and dashboards that built image URLs from it need another source.

How to get logos from a list of domains

  1. Open Company Logo & Favicon: Clearbit Logo Alternative on Apify.
  2. Paste your domains into Domains or URLs, for example python.org, apify.com. Duplicates and www/bare variants are merged.
  3. Run. Each site takes a few seconds.
  4. Export as CSV, JSON or Excel, or call the dataset API from your CRM or script. Use bestLogoUrl where you used the Clearbit URL, or bestIconUrl for small square avatars.

Example input:

{"domains": ["python.org", "apify.com"], "verifyLogo": true, "readManifest": true, "statusRows": true}

What you get per site

FieldWhat it is
bestLogoUrl, bestLogoSourceThe picked logo, in this order: JSON-LD Organization logo, a header logo image (footer images and logos linking to other sites are skipped), the largest app icon, og:image, favicon.
bestLogoVerifiedThe logo URL was fetched once and returned an image.
bestIconUrlSVG icon or the largest square icon, good for avatars and lists.
iconsEvery declared icon (rel=icon, apple-touch-icon, mask-icon, manifest icons) with sizes and type.
siteName, pageTitle, themeColor, finalUrlSite name, page title, brand theme color and the resolved site URL.

Price

Pay per result: $0.80 per 1,000 site results ($0.0008 per site with a logo or icon found) plus $0.00005 per run start. Sites that are blocked by robots.txt, redirect to another site, fail to load or have no logo get a free status row.

How it reads sites

  • Reads robots.txt first and obeys it.
  • Reads only the home page, the site's manifest file (optional) and one check of the chosen logo (optional): about 3 to 5 requests per site.
  • At least 1 second between requests to the same host, or the site's Crawl-delay. No cookies, no login, no JavaScript rendering.

Limits

  • Sites that build their head tags with JavaScript only may show fewer icons.
  • og:image is often a social banner, not a logo, so it ranks low in the pick.
  • Logos are trademarks of their owners. The tool returns the public URLs that sites publish; check your own use rights.
  • No e-mails, phone numbers or person names are collected.
