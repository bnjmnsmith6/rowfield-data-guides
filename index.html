Rowfield Data guides

Short how-to guides for common data tasks, each with the plain steps and the tool we built for it.

Disclosure: these are our own tools, published by Rowfield Data on Apify, built and operated with an AI agent.

Sitemap to CSV: extract every URL from a sitemap.xml

A free paste-in tool that turns sitemap XML into a URL list and a CSV file, right in your browser. For whole sites, gzipped sitemaps and nested indexes, use the Sitemap URL Extractor on Apify.

Clearbit Logo API alternative: get a company logo from a domain

Give a list of domains, get the best logo URL, a square icon, every declared favicon and app icon, the site name and the theme color. No API key from a logo vendor.

US state government jobs feed

Public job postings from the state hiring sites of California, New York, Wisconsin and North Dakota as one export with one row shape, for job boards, newsletters, databases and research, with a daily "only new" mode.

California state jobs data export (CalCareers)

California state government job postings from the public CalCareers search as a spreadsheet or API rows: working title, classification, department, location, telework, salary range, dates and link. For bulk data and research, not job alerts (CalCareers has its own).

Federal Register API to CSV

New US Federal Register rules, proposed rules, notices and presidential documents as one row each: agencies, dockets, CFR parts, comment deadlines and links to the official PDF.

EU tenders: TED data export

New EU calls for tender from TED (Tenders Electronic Daily) as JSON, CSV or Excel: notice number, title, buyer, country, CPV codes, publication date and links.

K-12 teacher and school district jobs data (AppliTrack)

Teacher, substitute, aide and other school job postings from 50 AppliTrack district portals in 36 states in one CSV, with the same columns for every district.

New York State job alerts: a daily feed of new StateJobsNY vacancies

A daily list of only the new New York State government vacancies from the public StateJobsNY table, filtered by title, agency or county.

Sitemap to Excel: convert an XML sitemap to a spreadsheet

A free paste-in tool that turns sitemap XML into a CSV file Excel opens directly. For whole sites, gzipped sitemaps and nested indexes, the Apify actor exports straight to Excel.

City and county bids and RFPs from CivicPlus sites as one CSV

Open bids, RFPs and RFQs from the Bid Postings pages of many CivicPlus city and county websites in one list: agency, state, bid number, title, deadline and link.

NVD CVEs to CSV, with CVSS, CWE and the CISA KEV flag

New or updated CVEs from the NIST NVD API as one row each: CVSS 4.0, 3.1 and 2.0 scores, severity, CWE ids, KEV flag, affected products and links.

SaaSHub alternatives export: competitor lists as rows

The ranked alternatives list for each product you name on SaaSHub, with votes, rating and the CC BY-SA 4.0 attribution fields on every row.

Sitemap extractors on Apify compared

Five Apify actors that turn a website's sitemaps into a URL list, side by side: price per 1,000 URLs (checked 2026-11-02), input and what each one does differently. Free options first.

Link preview API: title, description and og:image for a list of URLs

Free ways to read Open Graph tags first, then a pay-per-preview tool for bulk lists: title, description, og:image, Twitter card tags, favicon and canonical per URL. Failed URLs are free.

City and county government jobs from CivicPlus sites as one CSV

Current job postings from the public Jobs pages of many CivicPlus city and county websites in one list: agency, state, title, department, dates, salary when shown and link, with a daily "only new" mode.

Victoria government jobs data: Careers Vic listings as rows

Victorian Government (Australia) job listings from the public Careers Vic job board as one row each: title, organisation, work type, salary, grade, location, closing date and link, with a daily "only new" mode.

Manitoba government jobs feed: a daily list of new postings

Manitoba (Canada) government job postings from the province's public job feed: title, department, classification, salary, location, posted and closing dates and link, with a daily "only new" mode.

Wisconsin state jobs feed: wisc.jobs listings as rows

Wisconsin state government job listings from the public wisc.jobs list: title, agency, job class, category, location, posted and closing date and link, with a daily "only new" mode.

Canada provincial government jobs in one export

Public provincial government job postings from Manitoba, New Brunswick and Newfoundland and Labrador in one row shape with a province field, for job alerts, job boards and research, with a daily "only new" mode.

Latvia public sector vacancies (NVA vakances) as rows

Current Latvian public sector vacancies from the NVA open data set (CC0) with employer names added: title, category, salary range, workload, address, deadline and link, with a daily "only new" mode.

Empire Flippers listings export: online businesses for sale as rows

Online businesses for sale (or sold) on the Empire Flippers marketplace as one row each: asking price, multiples, net profit, revenue, niches, monetization and link, with a daily "only new" mode for deal alerts.

USGS earthquake data to CSV, one row per event

Earthquakes from the public USGS catalog as one row each: time, magnitude, place, coordinates, depth, PAGER alert, tsunami flag and link. Filter by date, magnitude, radius or box, with an "only new" mode.
