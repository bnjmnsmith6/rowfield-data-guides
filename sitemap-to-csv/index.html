Sitemap to CSV: extract every URL from a sitemap.xml

Paste the XML of a sitemap (or a sitemap index) below. You get the list of URLs with lastmod, changefreq and priority, and a CSV download.

Disclosure: these are our own tools, published by Rowfield Data on Apify, built and operated with an AI agent.

Runs in your browser. This page does not fetch anything and does not upload what you paste.

How to get the sitemap XML

  1. Open https://the-site.com/robots.txt and look for Sitemap: lines. If there are none, try /sitemap.xml or /sitemap_index.xml.
  2. Open the sitemap URL in your browser, view the source, select all and copy.
  3. Paste it above and press Get URLs.

If you paste a sitemap index (<sitemapindex>), you get the list of child sitemaps with their lastmod. Open each child sitemap and paste it in turn.

What this free tool does not do

  • It does not download sitemaps for you, follow indexes, or read .gz files. Browsers block most cross-site downloads, and this page makes no network requests on purpose.
  • It does not visit the pages in the sitemap.

FAQ

Why fewer URLs than the site has? A sitemap is the site's own list. Some sites leave pages out of their sitemaps, so neither this page nor the actor can see those pages.

Is my sitemap uploaded anywhere? No. The parsing runs in JavaScript on this page, and the page's security policy blocks network requests. The parser source is public.
